02 Sleeping & Dreaming

Sleeping & Dreaming

Circadian Rhythm

Definition: Biological clock that regulates sleep-wake cycles

Sleep Stages

Before Sleep

  • Experience hallucinations (falling or hearing your name)

Stage I

  • Duration: 5-10 minutes
  • Transition between wakefulness and sleep

Stage II

  • Duration: 20 minutes
  • Rhythmic brain waves
  • Lowered body temperature
  • Heart rate slows

Stage III

  • Transition between light and deep sleep

Stage IV (Deep Sleep)

  • Duration: 30 minutes
  • Sleepwalking, sleeptalking, and night terrors occur during this stage

REM Sleep

  • Duration: 15-45 minutes
  • Increased eye movement, breathing, and brain activity
  • Muscles relax
  • Dreaming, nightmares, and sleep paralysis occur

Sleep Cycle

  • Enter REM: Approximately 90 minutes into sleep
  • Return to Stage II
  • Cycles: 4-5 times per night
  • Pattern: First REM is short, but they get progressively longer
  • Pattern: Stage 4 becomes shorter as the night progresses

Sleep Disorders

Sleep Apnea

One or more pauses in breathing or shallow breaths while sleeping

Insomnia

Persistent problems falling and staying asleep

Narcolepsy

  • Chronic, overwhelming daytime drowsiness
  • Caused by brain's inability to regulate sleep-wake cycles normally

Dreaming

Basic Facts

  • Average: 4-7 dreams per night
  • Negative emotions are very common
  • Only dream about what we know (based on what we've seen before)

Daydreaming

Study (45 undergraduates)

Setup: 12-minute break, then "unusual use" creativity task
Groups:

  • Resting in quiet room
  • Difficult short-term memory task
  • Doing something boring
  • No break

Results: Boring task group came up with 41% more creative uses

Benefits

  • Allows for creativity and problem-solving
  • Enables mental "incubation" of problems

Lucid Dreaming

Definition: Dreaming while knowing you are dreaming

Potential Uses

  • Fantasy and adventure
  • Overcoming fears
  • Practice skills
  • Creativity and problem-solving
  • Mental health
  • Bonus: Beginning to notice details → higher level of awareness in awake state as well
