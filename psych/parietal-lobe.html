Parietal Lobe

Parietal Lobe

Location: Top and back of brain (behind frontal, above temporal)

Somatosensory Cortex

Definition: The region of parietal lobe that receives and processes touch sensations

Homunculus (Sensory Map)

What Gets Sensed?

Sensation Type Example Processed
Touch Texture of fabric Yes
Temperature Hot/cold Yes
Pressure Light touch vs. firm press Yes
Pain Sting, ache, burn Yes
Proprioception Where limbs are in space Yes

Visuospatial Processing

Functions

  • Spatial awareness: Knowing where you are
  • Navigation: Moving through space
  • Object location: Judging distances
  • Depth perception: 3D awareness

Body Schema

  • Mental representation of body position
  • Where limbs are in space
  • Continuously updated from proprioceptors

Damage Effects

  • Spatial neglect: Ignoring one side of space
  • Ataxia: Loss of coordination
  • Constructional apraxia: Can't build/draw

Contralateral Processing

Integration Hub

The parietal lobe is a sensory and spatial integration center:

  • Receives sensory input from thalamus
  • Processes multiple sensory types simultaneously
  • Integrates with visual information
  • Coordinates with motor areas for movement

Example: Touching Nose with Eyes Closed

  1. Touch sensations from hand (parietal)
  2. Proprioceptive feedback (parietal)
  3. Spatial location of nose (parietal)
  4. Motor command to move hand (frontal)
  5. Result: Successful coordination
