Carl Jung's Theory

Unconscious Levels

Archetypes

Definition: Inherited ideas based on ancestors' experiences that shape one's perception

How Archetypes Shape Personality

  1. Inherited patterns: Biological, pre-existing
  2. Activate unconsciously: Without awareness
  3. Guide perception: What we notice, how we interpret
  4. Influence behavior: Automatic responses
  5. Show in dreams: Symbolic representations
  6. Appear in myths: Universal story patterns

Personality Development

  • Integrating different archetypes
  • Balancing light and shadow
  • Achieving wholeness (individuation)
  • Becoming authentic self

Albert Bandura and Aggression

Bobo Doll Experiment

Research Question: Can aggression be learned through observation?

Setup

  • Participants: Young children
  • Condition 1: Watch adult behave aggressively toward doll
  • Condition 2: Watch adult behave calmly
  • Control: No observation

Observations

  1. Adult either punches, kicks, or sits quietly with doll
  2. Child watches (or doesn't)
  3. Child later placed in room with similar doll

Results

  • Observed aggression: Children were MORE aggressive
  • Specific actions: Imitated exact aggressive behaviors
  • No aggression condition: Children were calm

Implications

Social Learning Theory

  • Observation: Watch behavior
  • Encoding: Remember it
  • Reproduction: Try the behavior
  • Motivation: If rewarded/reinforced, repeat

Bandura's Personality Components

Triadic Reciprocality

  • Personality shapes how we see environment
  • Environment provides models and reinforcement
  • Behavior reflects personality and changes environment
  • All three constantly influencing each other

Criticisms of Psychoanalytic Theory

Strengths

  • ✓ Acknowledges unconscious processes
  • ✓ Emphasizes early experience importance
  • ✓ Considers defense mechanisms
  • ✓ Widely influenced psychology

Weaknesses

  • ✗ Difficult to test empirically
  • ✗ Based on clinical cases (biased sample)
  • ✗ Overemphasizes childhood
  • ✗ Limited scientific support
  • ✗ Some concepts outdated

Modern View

  • Psychoanalytic ideas influential but not dominant
  • Some concepts validated; others discarded
  • Defense mechanisms still studied
  • Unconscious processes confirmed by neuroscience
  • More emphasis on biology than Freud proposed
