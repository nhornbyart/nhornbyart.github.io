The Nervous System

The Nervous System

Function: Controls and coordinates all bodily functions

Major Divisions

Central Nervous System (CNS)

Components:

  • Brain: Control center for all functions
  • Spinal Cord: Connects brain to PNS; handles reflex actions

Peripheral Nervous System (PNS)

Function: Connects CNS to rest of body via branches of nerves

Somatic Nervous System

  • Controls voluntary movements
  • Transmits sensory info from skin, muscles, joints

Autonomic Nervous System

  • Controls involuntary functions
  • Heart rate, digestion, hormones

Sympathetic Division

  • Activation: Threat or stress
  • Response: Fight-or-flight
  • Increases: Heart rate, breathing, blood pressure
  • Decreases: Digestion, immune response

Parasympathetic Division

  • Activation: Safety and calm
  • Response: Rest-and-digest
  • Decreases: Heart rate, breathing
  • Increases: Digestion, immune response

Information Flow

Signal Transmission

Within a Neuron: Electrical

  • Action potentials travel along axon
  • Very fast transmission
  • All-or-nothing signal

Between Neurons: Chemical

  • Neurotransmitters cross synaptic gap
  • Slower than electrical
  • Can be modulated (amplified or inhibited)

Functions Controlled by Nervous System

Response Types

Reflex Arc (No Brain Required)

  • Fast, automatic response
  • Protects from immediate harm
  • Brain learns after action taken

Conscious Response

  • Signal reaches brain
  • Processing occurs
  • Voluntary decision made
  • Action taken
  • Slower but more flexible
