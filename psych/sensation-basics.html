Sensation Basics

Definition: When a stimulus activates the receptors of a sense organ; awareness of changing conditions.

What is a Stimulus?

A stimulus is something that causes a bodily response

Sensory Receptors

Stimulus Type Receptor Sense Organ
Light Photoreceptors Retina (eye)
Sound Hair cells Cochlea (ear)
Pressure Mechanoreceptors Skin
Chemicals Chemoreceptors Nose/Tongue
Temperature Thermoreceptors Skin

Key Distinction

  • Stimulus: Physical energy in the environment
  • Sensation: The conscious experience in the brain
  • Not all stimuli become sensations — many are filtered out!
