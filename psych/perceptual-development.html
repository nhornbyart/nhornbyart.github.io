Perceptual Development

Visual Cliff Study (Gibson & Walk, 1960)

Research Question: When do babies understand depth perception?

Experimental Setup

Procedure

  1. Mother sits on far side of "cliff"
  2. Baby placed on near side
  3. Observer watches baby's response
  4. Does baby crawl across apparent cliff?

Key Findings

Significance

  • Around 6 months: Baby develops depth perception
  • ~8 months: Mobile enough to notice/fear
  • First crawlers: More likely to show fear (understand danger)
  • Non-crawlers: May not show fear yet (can't move)

How Depth Perception Develops

Cues to Depth

Cue When Detected How
Binocular disparity 3-4 months Different image in each eye
Motion parallax 4-5 months Objects move at different speeds
Linear perspective 5-6 months Parallel lines appear to converge
Texture gradient 6-7 months Pattern density changes
Occlusion 4-5 months One object hides another

Other Perceptual Milestones

Visual Development

Auditory Development

  • 0-3 months: Responds to loud sounds
  • 3-6 months: Turns to familiar voices
  • 6-12 months: Recognizes own name
  • 12+ months: Understands simple words

Tactile Development

  • 0-3 months: Responds to touch, temperature
  • 3-6 months: Explores with mouth, hands
  • 6-12 months: Discriminates textures (soft, rough)

Fear Response Development

Why Fear Emerges

  1. Cognitive development: Understand danger
  2. Motor development: Can move toward danger
  3. Memory development: Remember bad experiences
  4. Pattern recognition: Recognize threatening stimuli

Common Fears by Age

Age Common Fears
6-9 months Strangers (stranger anxiety)
9-12 months Separation from parent
12-24 months Heights, loud noises
2-3 years Animals, darkness
4+ years Imaginary creatures, social situations

Implications for Parenting

Supporting Development

  • Provide safe exploration: Let baby explore safely
  • Gradual challenges: Introduce novel stimuli gradually
  • Reassurance: Be present during new experiences
  • Don't force: Respect baby's developmental readiness

Safety Awareness

  • Babies around 6-8 months develop fear of heights
  • Earlier: extra dangerous (no fear restraint)
  • Later: natural caution helps safety
  • Still need supervision (fear not foolproof)
