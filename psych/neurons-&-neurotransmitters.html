Neurons & Neurotransmitters

Neuron Structure

Key Neuron Components

Nucleus

  • Function: Stores and protects DNA
  • Produces: mRNA for protein synthesis
  • Makes: Proteins needed for neuron function

Cell Body (Soma)

  • Function: Creates energy for neuron
  • Contains: Mitochondria (powerhouses)
  • Integrates: Signals from dendrites

Myelin Sheath

  • Structure: Fatty insulation around axon
  • Function: Speeds up electrical impulses
  • Protection: Protects axon from damage
  • Benefit: Makes neural transmission 10-100x faster

Synaptic Terminals

  • Location: End of axon
  • Function: Release neurotransmitters
  • Structure: Vesicles containing neurotransmitters

Neural Communication

Neurotransmitters

Definition: Chemical messengers produced in neurons that transmit information between brain cells

Factors Affecting Levels

Balance is Key

  • Too much or too little linked to disorders
  • Medications often target neurotransmitter levels
  • Lifestyle affects neurotransmitter production

Neurotransmitter Imbalances & Disorders

Oversupply Problems

Acetylcholine

  • Excess linked to: Alzheimer's disease
  • Normal role: Learning, memory, movement
  • Problem: Buildup disrupts neural function

Dopamine

  • Excess linked to: Schizophrenia
  • Normal role: Motivation, reward, movement
  • Problem: Excessive dopamine = hallucinations, delusions

Undersupply Problems

Dopamine

  • Deficiency linked to: Parkinson's disease
  • Normal role: Motivation, movement control
  • Problem: Loss of motor control, reduced motivation

Serotonin

  • Deficiency linked to: Depression
  • Normal role: Mood regulation, sleep, appetite
  • Problem: Low mood, sleep disruption, anxiety

Synaptic Transmission

Balance

  • Excitatory signals encourage neuron firing
  • Inhibitory signals prevent neuron firing
  • Both types necessary for proper function
  • Too much excitation = seizures
  • Too much inhibition = paralysis/coma
