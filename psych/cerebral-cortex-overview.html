Cerebral Cortex Overview

Functional Specialization

Key Principle: Different functions are located in different parts of the brain

Historical Significance

Phineas Gage (1848): First case demonstrating localization of function

  • Metal tamping iron pierced through frontal lobe
  • Survived but personality changed dramatically
  • Showed frontal lobe involved in personality/behavior

Each Lobe at a Glance

Lobe Location Primary Functions
Frontal Front Planning, reasoning, movement, personality
Parietal Top/back Sensation, spatial awareness
Temporal Sides Hearing, memory, language comprehension
Occipital Back Vision

Organization Principles

Contralateral Control

  • Left hemisphere controls right side of body
  • Right hemisphere controls left side of body
  • Crossed neural pathways from brain stem

Distributed Processing

  • Complex functions involve multiple lobes
  • Example: Reading requires visual (occipital), language (temporal), motor (frontal)

Plasticity

  • Brain can reorganize after damage
  • Other areas can compensate for injuries
  • More plasticity in younger brains
