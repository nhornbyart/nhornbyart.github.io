Big Five Personality Model

Big Five Personality Model

Most well-known trait theory; most empirically supported

The OCEAN Model

Five central dimensions applicable to ALL people

The Five Traits

1. OPENNESS (O)

Spectrum: Open-minded ←→ Closed-minded

2. CONSCIENTIOUSNESS (C)

Spectrum: Responsible ←→ Careless

3. EXTRAVERSION (E)

Spectrum: Outgoing ←→ Reserved

4. AGREEABLENESS (A)

Spectrum: Sympathetic ←→ Cruel

5. NEUROTICISM (N) / EMOTIONAL STABILITY

Spectrum: Moody ←→ Calm/Relaxed

Personality Combinations

Unique Profiles

Everyone's Big Five score is different:

Example Profile 1:

O: High  C: High  E: Low  A: High  N: Low
= Creative, organized introvert; dependable, calm
(Good for: Research, writing, focused work)

Example Profile 2:

O: Low  C: Low  E: High  A: High  N: High
= Spontaneous, talkative; anxious, cooperative
(Good for: People-oriented work with support)

Predicting Behavior

Correlations with Life Outcomes

Trait Level Associated Outcomes
High Conscientiousness Better grades, job success, health
High Extraversion More friends, social success, but impulsive
High Agreeableness Better relationships, cooperative, but vulnerable
High Openness Creativity, learning, but indecision
High Neuroticism Mental health issues, anxiety, depression

Advantages of Big Five

Why It's Popular

  • ✓ Empirically supported: Most research of any model
  • ✓ Cross-cultural: Works across cultures
  • ✓ Stable: Traits remain relatively consistent
  • ✓ Comprehensive: Captures major personality variation
  • ✓ Practical: Used in business, clinical, research
  • ✓ Easy to measure: Standardized questionnaires
  • ✓ Predictive: Predicts important outcomes

Limitations

Criticisms

  • ✗ Doesn't explain why traits exist
  • ✗ Ignores situational influences
  • ✗ Self-report biases
  • ✗ May miss cultural-specific traits
  • ✗ Doesn't address values, motivations
  • ✗ Assumes stability (people change)
  • ✗ Reductionist (is personality really just these 5?)

Common Big Five Assessments

NEO-PI-R

  • 240 items
  • Most comprehensive
  • Research standard

Big Five Inventory (BFI)

  • 44 items
  • Shorter, practical
  • Widely used

OCEAN Tests

  • Various online versions
  • Quick assessment
  • Less precise than above

Workplace Applications

  • Used in hiring
  • Team building
  • Leadership development
  • Career counseling
