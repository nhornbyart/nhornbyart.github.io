03 Altered States of Consciousness

Altered States of Consciousness

Definition: Any condition which differs from a normal waking state
Note: Induced changes are almost always temporary

Biofeedback

Definition

Process of learning to control bodily states using specialized machines

How It Works

  • Machines tell a person about subtle bodily changes including:
    • Brain waves
    • Heart rate
    • Skin temperature
    • Sweat-gland activity
  • Person tries different thoughts and feelings while watching their effect on the body

Historical Research

Thomas Budzyński (1973):

  • Taught patients to relax muscles in the forehead to reduce tension headaches

Clinical Applications

  • Treating PTSD
  • ADHD
  • Depression
  • Anxiety

Question

Why is biofeedback considered an altered state of consciousness?

Meditation

Definition

Focusing of attention to clear one's mind and produce relaxation

Benefits

  • Lower blood pressure
  • Lower heart rate
  • Lower respiratory rate
  • Help with stress, insomnia, etc.

Types

Transcendental Meditation

  • Mental repetition of a mantra (e.g., Om)
  • Practice: Sit with eyes closed and meditate for 15-20 minutes 2× a day

Mindfulness Meditation

  • Focus on the present moment
  • Attention on: body, breath, and thoughts

Breath Meditation

  • Concentrate on breathing process
  • Provides focus and sense of calm

Hypnosis

Definition

Narrowed focus of attention; people become highly suggestible to changes in behavior and thought

How It Works

  1. Shifting Perceptions — Alter how reality is perceived
  2. Focus — On one small aspect of reality
  3. Relaxation — Forget outside anxiety
  4. Trust — Increase cooperation with suggestions

Uses of Hypnosis

Posthypnotic Suggestion

  • Suggestion made during hypnosis that influences behavior after the session
  • Helps to recall or suppress certain information

Hypnotic Analgesia

  • Reduction of pain
  • Mechanism: Helps patient relax and reduce anxiety → lessens perception of pain
  • Clinical use: Habits and phobias
