Sleep Stages

Circadian Rhythm

Biological clock that regulates sleep-wake cycles over approximately 24 hours

The Sleep Cycle

Cycle Pattern

  • Full cycle: ~90 minutes
  • Nightly cycles: 4-5 complete cycles
  • First REM: Short (5-10 min)
  • Last REM: Long (30-60 min)
  • Stage IV: Gets progressively shorter throughout night

Pre-Sleep

Experience hypnagogic hallucinations (hallucinations between waking and sleep)

  • Feeling of falling
  • Hearing your name called
  • Sleep starts/jerks

Stage I (Transition to Sleep)

Duration: 5-10 minutes

  • Bridge between wakefulness and sleep
  • Light sleep
  • Still easily awakened
  • Some muscle twitches

Stage II (Light Sleep)

Duration: 20 minutes per cycle

  • Sleep spindles: Brief bursts of rhythmic brain activity
  • Lowered body temperature
  • Heart rate slows
  • Still responsive to sounds

Stage III (Transition to Deep Sleep)

Duration: Variable

  • Bridge between light and deep sleep
  • Starting to enter deep sleep
  • Harder to wake

Stage IV (Deep Sleep)

Duration: 30 minutes

  • Deepest sleep stage
  • Hardest to wake from
  • Parasomnia occur here:
    • Sleepwalking
    • Sleep talking
    • Night terrors
    • Bedwetting

REM Sleep (Rapid Eye Movement)

Duration: 15-45 minutes per cycle (gets longer as night progresses)

Characteristics

  • Vivid dreams and nightmares
  • Sleep paralysis: Can't move (protective mechanism)
  • Heart rate and breathing increase
  • Brain wave activity resembles wakefulness
  • Body temperature regulation fails (feel hot or cold)

When REM Occurs

  • Enters first REM approximately 90 minutes into sleep
  • Returns to Stage II after REM
  • REM periods become progressively longer
  • Most vivid, longest dreams in final REM cycles
