Metalsmithing and Jewelry Design 2

Projects are organized in chronological order, from earliest to most recent. This page contains the course project gallery and a scaffolded artist statement you can edit to complete your portfolio requirements.

Chronological Projects

Circles Project

Three linked discs in mixed metals (sun, textured center disc, copper moon). Focused on texturing and clean joins in a connected composition.

Embellished Bezel Set Cabochon

Bezel-set cabochon within a layered brass frame. Practice in bezel forming, seating the stone, and adding decorative wirework for visual interest.

Resin Jewelry

Hexagon resin pendants with layered inclusions and spiral brass findings. Explored pours, layering, and finishing to reduce bubbles and get a glossy surface.
