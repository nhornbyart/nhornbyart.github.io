Projects are organized in chronological order, from earliest to most recent. This page contains the course project gallery and a scaffolded artist statement you can edit to complete your portfolio requirements.

Chronological Projects

Circles Project

Three linked metal discs, a sun, a textured center, and a copper moon. Practiced texturing and making neat joins.

Embellished Bezel Set Cabochon

A cabochon set in a round brass pendant with simple wire embellishments. Focus was on fitting the stone and tidying the finish.

Resin Jewelry

Two hexagon resin pendants filled with colorful sparkles and bits. I experimented with layering and getting a smooth, glossy finish.
